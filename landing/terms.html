1What this is

ReMed ("the Service") is a non-commercial side project run by AppsForLife ("we", "us", "me" — it's small). You send a photo or a typed description of a prescription to the ReMed bot on Telegram; automated processing (including AI models) drafts a medication schedule; and only after you explicitly confirm it does the Service send you reminders for each dose. The Service is free, doesn't create accounts, and exists to solve one small problem well.

2This is not medical advice

The Service is not a medical device and provides no medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not check what you send against any medical authority. The AI reading and scheduling can be and sometimes will be wrong — handwriting, abbreviations, lighting, and unusual formats all cause errors. You are responsible for verifying every line of the schedule against your actual prescription before confirming, and for consulting your doctor or pharmacist about anything medical. Never rely on ReMed alone for medication decisions.

The Service runs through your Telegram account, and your use of Telegram is governed by Telegram's own terms. We ask three simple things: don't send other people's prescriptions or personal data you aren't authorized to share; don't try to overload, break, or circumvent the Service's limits; and accept that we may rate-limit or block anyone who does otherwise. Because reminders depend on Telegram, your internet, and our little server, they may occasionally be late, missed, or duplicated.
