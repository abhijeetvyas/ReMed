How it works

From prescription to reminder
in under a minute.

01

Snap the prescription

Send a photo of the doctor's note — or type the medicines in your own words. ReMed's AI reads names, dosage, food timing and duration.

02

Check the schedule

Nothing runs until you've seen exactly what was read. Confirm every line — or fix any of it in plain words, like "Dolo is actually 5 days".

03

Get reminded. Tap ✅.

One well-timed nudge per dose, built around your own meal times — until the course ends. Mark Taken or Skip with a tap, and it knows.
